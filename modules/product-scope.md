# Product Scope

**MODE:** NATIVE

## Trigger

Project direction is known but the valuable behavior, boundaries or priority are not.

## Purpose

Choose the smallest coherent product outcome worth building.

## Requires

- relevant `PROJECT.md` sections;
- discovery output or equivalent evidence.

## Optional

Current product behavior, user evidence, constraints and competing alternatives.

## Do not load

Detailed implementation plans or unrelated future ideas.

## Procedure

1. Define the user outcome and the end-to-end moment of value.
2. Build the gap ledger and resolve `RESEARCHABLE` gaps through `research` before fixing scope.
3. Separate must-have behavior from enhancements and non-goals.
4. Prefer a narrow complete wedge over a broad partial platform.
5. Apply the narrowness check once: is this the narrowest **useful** outcome, or merely the narrowest? Widen only when the smaller version cannot be observed as valuable by its user.
6. Test scope against success, constraints and operational reality.
7. Resolve reversible choices automatically; escalate only gaps passing the promotion test.
8. Patch `PROJECT.md`, declare project traits, and seed `SPEC.md` boundaries.

Every other gate in this framework can only remove scope. Step 5 is the only one that may add it, and it fires once.

## Exit criteria

- in-scope and out-of-scope behavior are clear;
- the first useful outcome is independently deliverable;
- the wedge passed the narrowness check;
- success can be observed;
- no speculative subsystem is required.

## Outputs

Updated `PROJECT.md`; scope section in `SPEC.md`; decisions if durable.
