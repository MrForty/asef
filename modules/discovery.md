# Discovery

**MODE:** NATIVE

## Trigger

`GREENFIELD` or material uncertainty about the problem, user or desired outcome.

## Purpose

Turn an incomplete idea into a sufficiently evidenced project identity without conducting an unnecessary interview.

## Requires

- user request;
- existing `PROJECT.md`, if any.

## Optional

- current product or code evidence;
- `research` for market and competing-solution evidence.

## Do not load

Implementation details, full repository, unrelated research or downstream plans.

## Procedure

1. Extract known problem, users, status quo, desired outcome, constraints and the domain terms the user already uses.
2. Build the gap ledger for every material unknown that remains.
3. Resolve `RESEARCHABLE` gaps through `research`: competing solutions, market status quo, constraints. First-party demand evidence is never routed here.
4. Identify the narrowest useful wedge and observable success signal.
5. Ask surviving `USER-DECISION` gaps: demand, status quo and wedge one at a time under the demand exemption; everything else in one round.
6. Record material assumptions with a validation path and revisit trigger.
7. Create or patch `PROJECT.md`: traits, domain terms, assumptions.

A first answer about demand is usually the rehearsed one. Before recording it as `FACT`, push for the specific person, the specific occasion, and what the user did rather than said. Interest, praise and stated intent are not demand.

## Exit criteria

- problem and target user are specific enough to guide scope;
- desired outcome and success evidence are observable;
- critical constraints and assumptions are explicit;
- demand evidence is first-party and specific, or left `OPEN` rather than defaulted;
- no unresolved question blocks product scoping.

## Outputs

`PROJECT.md`; durable decisions when necessary; compact `STATE.md` update.

## Next

`product-scope` or `DONE` if discovery alone was requested.
